    About this prototype

    What it is. HOLDLINE is a hackathon prototype that puts blood units on hold after a fridge temperature problem and records who decided what. It is not a medical device and has not been reviewed by clinicians. Thresholds are chosen settings, not clinical limits.

    Data. Every facility, unit, reading and user here is synthetic. Demo users are role names with public demo PINs.

    Privacy. Nothing is collected or sent anywhere else. No analytics, no cookies, no third-party requests. This browser keeps the last data, your sign-in token, filters and any decision waiting to sync in local storage; signing out removes the token. SMS messages are previews only; nothing is sent.

    Who can do what. A duty officer can keep a hold. A release needs a qualified releaser and a second qualified releaser's PIN, and a final slide to confirm.

    Open-source parts. Inter and IBM Plex Mono (SIL Open Font License 1.1). Four Tabler outline icons (MIT). No other third-party code. Details in THIRD_PARTY.md in the repository.
