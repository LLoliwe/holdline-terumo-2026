In range

Fridge 2 to 6°C, logger reporting. Red cells and whole blood.

Excursion

Above 6°C in a fridge, or above 10°C in a cool box. Exposed units are held at once.

Unverified

Logger gap or sensor glitch (-99.0, 99.9, 0.0). We don't know, so we say so.

Verified

A named person checked it and signed. Shows who and when.

On hold

Unit can't be issued until a qualified person releases or discards it.

Rules
  1. The system holds. Only a person releases. No unit is ever released, discarded or matched by the software alone.
  2. Show the reading, not a score. Every flag carries the temperatures, the hours and the logger that produced it.
  3. Gaps are honest. Missing data is drawn as hatched, never smoothed over.
  4. Built for the bench. 44px touch targets, works at 360px wide, and every alert also fits in one SMS.
  5. Settings, not code. Thresholds, language and site names come from a country file.
